Our Services

We offer both ongoing monthly support and one-time financial services tailored to the needs of your establishment. Our goal is to provide you with clear insights into your business's financial performance and help you make informed decisions.


Pricing is based on the average monthly turnover of your business. Contact us for a free consultation, and together we will identify the most suitable solution for your needs. This consultation, includes a first asessment of your personal business needs and our recommendation of the most suitable solution.

Working with your systems

All services are provided using your existing accounting and inventory systems, so there is no need to purchase additional software or tools.

We work with all standard financial and accounting tools — from local solutions to international programs. We’ll adopt your existing structure or set up a new one as needed to ensure you receive clear, reliable reports.

Monthly Services

Our monthly services are designed for businesses that want to maintain ongoing control over their food and beverage costs, inventory, and overall financial performance. We provide regular reporting, analysis, and recommendations to help you optimize your operations and increase profitability.

stacked_bar_chart

Basic package – Food Cost and Beverage Cost Control

For businesses that want to keep product costs and inventory management under control.

Includes

  • Recording purchase invoices
  • Recording write-offs
  • Conducting inventory counts
  • Inventory stock control
  • Verification of recipe cards
  • Calculation of actual Food Cost
  • Calculation of actual Beverage Cost
  • Monitoring Food Cost and Beverage Cost variances
  • Monthly report on variances, losses, and costs

Result

You know the actual cost of dishes and beverages, control inventory levels, and identify variances and losses in a timely manner.

Advanced package – Profit Control and Financial Support

For owners who want to understand the financial condition of the business and make decisions based on numbers.

Includes

  • All services from the Basic package PLUS:
  • Calculation of the venue’s profit
  • Cash flow control and cash gap monitoring
  • Expense structure analysis
  • Menu category profitability analysis and menu engineering
  • Procurement analysis and identification of opportunities to reduce costs
  • Monthly business performance analysis and recommendations for increasing profit

Result

You understand how much the business actually earns, where money is being lost, and which actions will help increase profitability and operational efficiency.
